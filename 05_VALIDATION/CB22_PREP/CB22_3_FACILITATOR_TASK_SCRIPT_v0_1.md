# CB22 Facilitator Task Script v0.1

Status: DRAFT — FOR APPROVED STUDY USE ONLY

## Standard opening

Explain that SIFI is a local analytical prototype. The exercise evaluates the
workflow, not the participant. Outputs must not be treated as factual or used
for institutional action. The participant may stop at any time under the
approved consent terms.

Confirm the participant code, protocol version, task-package version, and
information cutoff. Do not reveal expected conclusions.

## Task instruction

Ask the participant to inspect the provided case through every visible SIFI
stage, record any needed analytical notes, and finish the evidence-linked
brief. Ask them to work as they normally would within the stated boundaries.

## During-task conduct

- Do not coach normal navigation or interpretation.
- If safety or data-integrity intervention is necessary, intervene and log it.
- Log every participant request for help, whether or not help is provided.
- Do not silently repair application state.
- Do not convert attention into materiality or supply analytical conclusions.
- Stop the session on consent withdrawal, unsafe use, or protocol breach.

## Standard closing

Record completion state, end time, application failures, interventions, and
any forbidden output. Collect structured blocker feedback without asking for
direct identifiers. Do not tell the participant whether the session “passed.”
Only the approved adjudication process determines the aggregate CB22 result.
