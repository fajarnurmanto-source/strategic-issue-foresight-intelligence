# CB22 Participant and Consent Gate v0.1

Status: TEMPLATE — REQUIRES STUDY-AUTHORITY APPROVAL

Before a counted session, an authorized coordinator must confirm all items:

- participant matches the frozen intended-analyst profile;
- participant did not build or test the evaluated workflow;
- participation is voluntary and withdrawal terms were explained;
- purpose, task, expected duration, and foreseeable burden were explained;
- locally captured data fields and their purpose were explained;
- retention, access, deletion, and reporting rules were explained;
- consent was recorded through the approved mechanism;
- a pseudonymous participant code was issued;
- no direct identifier will be entered into SIFI;
- the participant received the same approved task package and cutoff;
- conflicts, accommodations, and prior SIFI exposure were recorded;
- the session is marked pilot or counted before it begins.

Required external records (references only inside study data):

- screening record ID;
- consent record ID;
- governance authorization ID;
- protocol version;
- task-package version.

This template does not itself constitute consent or governance approval.
