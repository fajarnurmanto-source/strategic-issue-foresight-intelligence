# CB22 Consent and Screening Form v1.0

Use this form through the authorized study process. Do not store the completed
form or direct identifiers in SIFI. Enter only its pseudonymous record code in
the local facilitator interface.

## Information to provide

The session evaluates whether the SIFI workflow is usable. It evaluates the
software, not the participant. Participation is voluntary. The participant may
stop without penalty. Structured observations include workflow completion,
time, coded interventions, failures, blockers, and evidence-link checks. SIFI
does not need the participant's name, contact details, employer, signature, or
free-text statements.

## Screening checklist

- Participant meets every frozen inclusion criterion: YES / NO
- Any frozen exclusion criterion applies: YES / NO
- Any applicable organizational/ethics requirement remains unmet: YES / NO
- Assigned pseudonymous participant code: __________________
- Assigned screening record code: __________________

Eligibility requires YES, NO, NO in that order.

## Consent checklist

- Study purpose and procedure explained: YES / NO
- Voluntary participation and withdrawal explained: YES / NO
- Data categories, retention, and access explained: YES / NO
- Questions answered: YES / NO
- Participant affirmatively agrees before timing begins: YES / NO
- Assigned consent record code: __________________
- Consent confirmation timestamp with timezone: __________________

All answers must be YES. The authorized custodian retains this form according
to the applicable process; SIFI receives only codes and the confirmation time.
