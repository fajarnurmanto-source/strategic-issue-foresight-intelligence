# CB22 Representative Participant Recruitment Runbook v1.0

Status: READY FOR AUTHORIZED HUMAN COORDINATION — NO PARTICIPANT RECRUITED BY THIS ARTIFACT

## Target

Recruit at least seven prospective participants to provide margin for screening,
withdrawal, and scheduling, while stopping only after at least five valid
counted sessions are finalized. Do not recruit SIFI developers, workflow
authors, threshold setters, or people who do not meet every frozen inclusion
criterion.

Suitable profiles include current or recent issue-monitoring, research-
synthesis, policy-analysis, risk-analysis, or intelligence-analysis work with
routine desktop-browser use. Recruitment must not imply that individual job
performance is being evaluated.

## Contact and identity boundary

An authorized human coordinator performs outreach through an approved channel.
Names, email addresses, phone numbers, employers, calendars, and identity-code
mappings remain outside SIFI under the applicable process. SIFI receives only
codes such as `P-001`, `SCR-001`, and `CON-001`.

Do not paste a contact list into the SIFI folder, Codex thread, dashboard,
terminal, logs, source code, or Git history.

## Neutral invitation text

> We are evaluating the usability of a local analytical workflow using a
> fictional evidence package. The session evaluates the software, not your job
> performance. Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time. The
> study takes place on the coordinator's local computer and records only a
> pseudonymous participant code plus structured workflow observations. Please
> ask the coordinator for the approved information and consent material before
> deciding whether to participate.

The coordinator may translate this text without adding benefit claims,
pressure, production-readiness claims, or promises that SIFI has been proven.

## Sequence

1. Resolve and document the applicable external/institutional review status.
2. Assign two distinct adjudication roles and complete conflict review.
3. Prepare at least seven prospective contacts outside SIFI.
4. Provide the approved study information before screening or scheduling.
5. Apply every inclusion and exclusion criterion consistently.
6. Assign pseudonymous codes only after eligibility is determined.
7. Obtain affirmative individual consent before opening a timed session.
8. Run one rehearsal with a non-counted eligible volunteer or facilitator.
9. Execute counted sessions without changing protocol, task, or thresholds.
10. Document withdrawals and scheduling failures outside analytical outcomes;
    never replace a poor outcome selectively.

## Prohibited recruitment practices

- coercion, undisclosed incentives, or manager pressure;
- claiming that participation is required for employment or access;
- targeting vulnerable people without an approved safeguard;
- collecting identities in SIFI;
- screening after inspecting participant outcomes;
- recruiting only known supporters of the product;
- counting the same participant twice.
