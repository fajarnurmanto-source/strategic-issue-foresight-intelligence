Information to provide / Informasi

The session evaluates whether the SIFI workflow is usable. It evaluates the software, not the participant. Participation is voluntary. The participant may stop without penalty. Structured observations include workflow completion, time, coded interventions, failures, blockers, and evidence-link checks. SIFI does not need the participant's name, contact details, employer, signature, or free-text statements. Sesi menilai kegunaan alur kerja SIFI; yang dinilai adalah software, bukan Anda. Sukarela, boleh berhenti kapan saja. Observasi mencakup penyelesaian workflow, waktu, kode intervensi, kegagalan, kendala, dan evidence-link. SIFI tidak butuh nama, kontak, employer, atau tanda tangan Anda di dalam sistem.
