Static contract regression does not establish L2.

At Stage0:

- verified contract architecture may reach L1;
- executable behaviour is required for L2;
- functional test evidence is required for L3;
- complete workflow evidence is required for L4;
- representative-user evidence is required for L5;
- comparative decision evidence is required for L6.

## 13. Product Workability vs Incremental Decision Value

SIFI preserves two independent verification tracks.

TRACK A — PRODUCT WORKABILITY

asks whether SIFI can correctly execute the bounded intended workflow.

TRACK B — INCREMENTAL DECISION VALUE

asks whether using SIFI materially improves the quality, timeliness,
calibration, or defensibility of a relevant decision compared with a
credible baseline under comparable information and rights conditions.

Track A PASS does not imply Track B PASS.

Functional correctness, end-to-end operability, analyst usability, and
decision superiority must not be collapsed into one claim.

A product may be operationally usable without producing incremental
decision value.

Decision-value claims therefore remain unverified until comparative
evidence exists.
