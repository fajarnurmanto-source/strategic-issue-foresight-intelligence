# SIFI S0.2 — Product Contract Draft

Status: WORKING DRAFT — NOT FROZEN
Stage: 0 — Foundation
Authority: definition only; implementation remains unauthorized

## 1. Product Identity
SIFI is a standalone Strategic Issue & Foresight Intelligence product that periodically collects publicly accessible external information and transforms it into auditable Issue Objects and, later, reviewable Foresight Objects.

SIFI is not:
- a generic media-monitoring dashboard;
- a raw social-listening tool;
- an automated policy-decision system;
- a causal inference engine by default;
- a replacement for Planning Intelligence (PI) or Investment Intelligence (II).

## 2. Primary Product Question
What is emerging or changing in the external environment, how strong is the evidence, what trajectory is visible, what risks or opportunities may follow, and what conditions should be watched next?

## 3. Primary Product Output
The primary product output is a persistent Issue Object with:
- canonical identity and aliases;
- geography and domain;
- lifecycle state;
- trajectory dimensions;
- supporting and contradictory evidence;
- evidence state and uncertainty;
- affected population/exposure;
- reversibility and time-to-impact;
- drivers, leading indicators, horizon, and watch conditions;
- human review state and audit history.

Foresight Objects are a later-stage derivative and may not be treated as free-form predictions.

## 4. Epistemic Contract
SIFI must preserve the distinction between:
1. detected attention;
2. reported claim;
3. corroborated empirical condition;
4. causal finding;
5. strategic implication;
6. foresight hypothesis.

Media volume or repetition is not empirical truth and must never silently promote an issue into a factual or causal finding.

## 5. AI Authority
AI may assist extraction, clustering, classification, summarization, hypothesis generation, and watch-condition proposals.
AI may not:
- invent absent facts;
- irreversibly merge evidence without audit trail;
- convert a model label into an unquestioned strategic finding;
- create unsupported causal explanations;
- present speculation as forecast certainty;
- make binding policy or investment decisions.

## 6. Commercial Position
Initial entry category: issue/media intelligence.
Differentiation path:
1. cleaner, deduplicated, auditable issue intelligence;
2. recurrence and structural-issue detection;
3. evidence corroboration and contradiction handling;
4. structured foresight and watch conditions;
5. reusable Issue API for PI, II, and enterprise systems.

## 7. Interoperability Boundary
SIFI produces external-intelligence objects and evidence packages.
PI consumes SIFI to test plans, assumptions, interventions, and public value.
II consumes SIFI for sector, company, macro, regulatory, and reputation-risk signals.
SIFI does not inherit PI or II decision authority.

## 8. Pilot Optimization Persona
Primary pilot persona:
Government communications / public-information analyst or team lead who currently performs recurring manual issue monitoring and briefing.

Secondary validation persona:
Planning/research users such as Bappeda/Bapperida/BRIDA analysts.

The pilot workflow is optimized for the primary persona first; secondary personas may validate transferability but must not broaden the MVP contract.

## 9. Success Contract
Stage-1/V1 success is not defined by crawler volume or dashboard appearance.
The product must demonstrate:
- reproducible source acquisition;
- complete provenance for accepted evidence items;
- inspectable deduplication decisions;
- persistent Issue Objects across cycles;
- explicit separation of attention from corroboration;
- visible coverage gaps and source health;
- human correction/qualification;
- materially faster and more consistent briefing than the current manual workflow.

Quantitative thresholds for time saved, correction rate, clustering quality, and coverage are NOT frozen here. They must be calibrated from the Stage-0 benchmark and a measured manual baseline.

## 10. Freeze Rule
This document is a working contract. It may be frozen only after the pilot boundary, source/rights model, evidence model, ontology, benchmark corpus, acceptance tests, and human-review authority are mutually consistent.
