# SIFI S0.3W3 — Successor Integration Record

Status: ACTIVE CONTROL RECORD

## 1. Predecessor Preservation

S0.3W does not replace or rewrite:

- S0.3A-D Source Governance Core;
- S0.3R Geography/Relevance Contract;
- S0.3S Coverage/Profile controls;
- S0.3T Geographic Relevance Decision Contract;
- S0.3U Source Registry and Rights Governance;
- S0.3V Current-State Gap Audit.

## 2. Additive Responsibility

S0.3U answers primarily:

What is the source, how may it be accessed, and what source-level rights and
retention constraints apply?

S0.3W adds:

Can this specific material support research only, production evidence, or
derived production output under the intended use?

## 3. Authority Separation

Source verification status must not be treated as equivalent to:

- evidence admission;
- geographic relevance;
- evidence strength;
- issue importance.

## 4. Non-Duplication Decision

Do not create a replacement Source Registry merely to add admission zones.

Future registry extension should be additive and schema-compatible.

source-rights audit.

S0.3Y:

S0.3Z:
