# SIFI S0.3X3 — Source Rights & Retention Acceptance Matrix v0.1

Status: WORKING BENCHMARK — NOT FROZEN

## CASE-01 — Public HTML Without Clear Commercial Rights

Expected:

research/discovery may be possible if otherwise lawful;
production rights remain UNKNOWN;
candidate zone = Z1;
no default full-text retention.

## CASE-02 — Open-Licensed Official Dataset

Expected:

licence is recorded;
commercial/derived/redistribution scope is evaluated separately;
permitted retention class is explicit;
candidate production eligibility may reach Z2.

## CASE-03 — robots.txt Allows Crawling

Expected:

technical crawl signal only.

Must not be interpreted as:
commercial-use permission,
redistribution permission,
or full-text retention permission.

## CASE-04 — robots.txt Restricts Relevant Route

Expected:

restriction is recorded and respected.

No bypass.

## CASE-05 — Browser Needed Because Static Route Fails

Expected:

browser automation is not automatically executed.

State:
BROWSER_REQUIRED_PENDING_RIGHTS_REVIEW.

## CASE-06 — Article Full Text Not Clearly Retainable

Expected:

prefer R0/R1/R2/R4 as applicable.

Do not default to R3_FULLTEXT_PERMITTED.

## CASE-07 — Facts May Be Analysed, Expression May Not Be Republished

Expected:

factual extraction and protected expression remain distinct.

Customer output should cite, summarise and link.

## CASE-08 — Dataset Has Database-Reuse Restriction

Expected:

database-right risk recorded separately from ordinary copyright.

Permitted factual use must not silently authorise bulk database reuse.

## CASE-09 — Terms Change After Earlier Acquisition

Expected:

new rights state is versioned.

Historical rights state remains point-in-time identifiable.

No retroactive silent mutation.

## CASE-10 — Licence Expires

Expected:

new ingestion stops at effective loss of rights;
source becomes SUSPENDED/REVOKED as appropriate;
historical treatment is evaluated separately.

## CASE-11 — Source Disappears

Expected:

historical provenance is not automatically invalidated.

Retention depends on the rights governing previously acquired material.

## CASE-12 — Profile Switch

Expected:

rights state is unchanged solely because CoverageProfile changes.

Geographic relevance may change.

## CASE-13 — Public Social Post

Expected:

this contract alone is insufficient for production admission.

Dedicated social/privacy control is required.

## CASE-14 — Acquisition Cost Is High

Expected:

cost does not itself prohibit admission.

Cost is retained for later Source Value / Evidence Efficiency analysis.

## CASE-15 — Technically Accessible Private Endpoint

Expected:

technical reachability does not create authority.

If access is unauthorised:
Z0 / BLOCKED.

## CASE-16 — No Terms Found

Expected:

absence of located terms does not equal unrestricted permission.

Material production rights remain UNKNOWN.

## CASE-17 — Explicit Written Permission

Expected:

permission locator, scope, effective period and reviewer are recorded.

Permission applies only within its documented scope.

## CASE-18 — Implementation Guard

Expected:

S0.3X-A PASS does not authorise crawler activation.

Implementation Authority remains NOT YET AUTHORIZED.
