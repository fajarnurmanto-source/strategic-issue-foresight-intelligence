# SIFI S0.3W1 — Data Rights & Evidence Admission Successor v0.1

Status: WORKING SUCCESSOR — NOT FROZEN

## 1. Purpose

This successor introduces an explicit control plane between source discovery
and production evidence.

It is additive and non-destructive.

It does not replace:

- Source Governance Core;
- S0.3U Source Registry;
- S0.3U Rights & Access Audit Contract;
- SourceCoverageBinding;
- Geography/Relevance contracts;
- CoverageProfile;
- existing provenance controls.

## 2. Permanent Invariants

### I1 — Accessible Is Not Production-Usable

Technical or public accessibility does not establish permission for:

- recurring automated acquisition;
- full-text retention;
- redistribution;
- customer-facing use;
- commercial use;
- training use;
- derived use.

### I2 — Research Signal Is Not Production Evidence

A signal may inform search or verification without being admitted into
canonical production evidence.

### I3 — Geographic Relevance Is Not Rights Admissibility

An item may be highly relevant to a CoverageProfile and still be inadmissible
as production evidence.

Likewise, production-admitted evidence may still be irrelevant to a given
CoverageProfile.

## 3. Orthogonal Decision Axes

SIFI must preserve separate decisions for:

1. source technical accessibility;
2. source rights status;
3. evidence admission;
4. evidence reliability / corroboration;
5. geographic relevance;
6. issue significance;
7. privacy eligibility;
8. downstream output eligibility.

No single score may collapse these axes.

## 4. Data / Source Zones

### Z0 — PROHIBITED

Material must not be acquired, retained, processed, analysed, or used as
evidence when acquisition or processing is prohibited or unauthorised.

Examples include:

- stolen or breached datasets;
- stolen credentials;
- hacked material acquired through prohibited access;
- private messages without authority;
- private groups without authority;
- paywall bypass;
- CAPTCHA bypass;
- login bypass;
- defeated anti-bot controls;
- hidden/private endpoints accessed without authority;
- unlawfully obtained personal or location data;
- confidential institutional information obtained without authority.

Allowed control behaviour:

SIFI may retain a non-payload governance record stating that a source or
route is BLOCKED.

The prohibited payload itself must not be retained.

### Z1 — RESEARCH_ONLY

Material may be lawfully accessible for limited research/discovery use, but
one or more material production rights remain unresolved or unsuitable.

Typical conditions:

- commercial use unclear;
- retention unclear;
- redistribution unclear;
- derived-use right unclear;
- production use not established;
- privacy eligibility unresolved;
- source is useful for discovery but not production evidence.

Z1 may support:

- source evaluation;
- parser testing where permitted;
- signal discovery;
- hypothesis generation;
- verification routing;
- feature exploration;
- benchmark research where permitted.

Z1 must not independently support:

- canonical production evidence;
- customer-facing findings;
- issue escalation;
- risk labels;
- recommendations;
- automated actionable conclusions.

### Z2 — PRODUCTION_ADMITTED

Material may enter canonical production evidence only when the intended
production use has an adequate rights and governance basis.

Minimum conditions:

- lawful acquisition path;
- relevant rights state is known;
- required commercial-use permission is satisfied where applicable;
- retention mode is permitted;
- provenance is traceable;
- attribution obligations are representable;
- source/evidence identity is reproducible;
- material restrictions are recorded;
- privacy gate is satisfied where applicable;
- evidence is not produced through a prohibited acquisition path.

Z2 does not imply:

- factual truth;
- high evidence strength;
- strategic importance;
- geographic relevance.

Those remain separate determinations.

### Z3 — DERIVED_OUTPUT

Z3 contains derived SIFI outputs, including:

- Issue Objects;
- relevance assessments;
- trajectories;
- risk/opportunity states;
- foresight hypotheses;
- scenarios;
- watch conditions;
- escalation/de-escalation states;
- decision-support outputs.

Z3 inherits applicable restrictions from supporting evidence.

Derived output is not automatically unrestricted merely because it is
generated by SIFI.

## 5. Source-Level vs Evidence-Level Admission

Source-level approval is necessary but may not always be sufficient.

SIFI must distinguish:

SOURCE RIGHTS ELIGIBILITY

from:

EVIDENCE ITEM ADMISSION.

A registered source may contain:

- permitted material;
- restricted material;
- different datasets with different licences;
- different endpoints with different terms;
- content involving different privacy risks.

Therefore production admission must preserve the scope of the decision.

## 7. Admission States

Minimum admission states:

- NOT_EVALUATED
- RESEARCH_SIGNAL
- VERIFICATION_REQUIRED
- PRODUCTION_ADMITTED
- PRODUCTION_REJECTED
- BLOCKED
- SUSPENDED
- REVOKED
- SUPERSEDED

Admission state and admission zone are related but not interchangeable.

Example:

admission_zone = Z1
admission_state = VERIFICATION_REQUIRED

## 8. Promotion Path

The controlled promotion path is:

RAW SIGNAL
→ CANDIDATE SIGNAL
→ CORROBORATED SIGNAL
→ ISSUE CANDIDATE
→ QUALIFIED ISSUE
→ STRATEGIC ISSUE
→ WATCH / ESCALATE / DE-ESCALATE.

Promotion requires explicit checks for:

- rights/admission;
- provenance;
- source independence;
- corroboration;
- geographic relevance;
- temporal validity;
- contradiction;
- uncertainty;
- evidence sufficiency.

## 9. Research Signal Rule

A Z1 signal may trigger searches for:

- official confirmation;
- permitted primary source;
- regulator statement;
- independent journalism;
- statistical or open-data evidence;
- other production-admitted corroboration.

The Z1 signal itself must not silently become Z2.

Promotion requires a new explicit admission decision.

## 10. Weak Signal Rule

Weak signals may justify:

- investigation;
- watchlisting;
- evidence search;
- analyst review.

Weak signals alone must not automatically create:

- strategic-issue status;
- high-risk status;
- customer recommendation;
- causal conclusion;
- operational escalation.

Novelty is not equivalent to importance.

## 11. Geographic Relevance Separation

Rights admission and geographic relevance are orthogonal.

Example:

An item may be:

relevance_class = R0
admission_zone = Z1

Meaning:

directly relevant to the active geography,
but not admissible as production evidence.

Another item may be:

relevance_class = RX
admission_zone = Z2

Meaning:

lawfully usable evidence,
but irrelevant to the active profile.

## 12. Unknown Is Non-Permissive

Unknown material rights must not silently become permission.

If material production-use rights are unresolved:

production admission is denied or held in Z1.

If the unresolved question is not material to the intended use,
the reasoning must be explicit and reviewable.

## 13. Security Bypass Hard Block

SIFI must not use:

- credential theft;
- credential reuse without authority;
- CAPTCHA bypass;
- paywall bypass;
- login bypass;
- anti-bot defeat;
- hidden/private endpoint exploitation;
- security-control circumvention.

Browser automation is not permission.

Technical capability is not authorization.

## 14. Production Hard Blocks

Production evidence must be blocked where any applicable condition exists:

- Z0 material;
- prohibited acquisition route;
- material rights unknown for intended commercial use;
- retention prohibited for required storage mode;
- provenance materially untraceable;
- required attribution cannot be satisfied;
- rights permission expired or revoked;
- privacy processing is unlawful or unresolved where material;
- individual profiling is unapproved where material;
- founder/employment-privileged data lacks independent authorization.

No silent override is permitted.

## 15. Override Governance

Where an override mechanism is legally and operationally permissible, it must
be:

- explicit;
- named;
- justified;
- timestamped;
- scoped;
- logged;
- reviewable;
- reversible.

An override may not authorize a prohibited acquisition path.

## 16. Point-in-Time Requirement

Admission decisions must preserve:

- rights state effective at acquisition;
- source registry version;
- admission contract version;
- review timestamp;
- later suspension/revocation events.

Historical evidence must remain reconstructable without pretending later
rights states existed earlier.

## 17. Revocation Rule

If a source or evidence loses production eligibility:

- stop new production ingestion under the invalid permission;
- move future admission state to SUSPENDED or REVOKED;
- preserve the reason and effective time;
- apply retention/deletion requirements to historical material;
- disclose resulting coverage loss.

Historical evidence must not be silently erased or silently retained contrary
to governing requirements.

## 18. Derived Output Carry-Through

Z3 outputs must retain sufficient lineage to determine:

- which Z2 evidence supports them;
- whether supporting evidence remains valid;
- attribution obligations;
- source restrictions;
- privacy restrictions;
- contradiction state;
- evidence gaps.

A Z3 output may not hide that its supporting evidence has been revoked,
restricted, or materially weakened.

## 19. AI Authority

AI may:

- propose admission candidates;
- identify potential rights questions;
- classify candidate signals;
- propose verification routes;
- summarise admitted evidence;
- propose issue promotion for review.

AI may not:

- create permission;
- override source rights;
- convert Z1 into Z2 without controlled admission;
- bypass security controls;
- infer that public availability equals commercial permission;
- hide unresolved rights or privacy risk.

## 20. Implementation Guard

This contract does not authorize production implementation.

Until downstream controls are completed:

- no new crawler capability is authorised;
- no expanded social collection is authorised;
- no individual profiling is authorised;
- no paid-production evidence pipeline is authorised.

Implementation Authority remains:

NOT YET AUTHORIZED.
