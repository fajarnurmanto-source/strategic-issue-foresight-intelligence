# SIFI S0.5A2 — Claim–Evidence Qualification Matrix v0.1

Status: WORKING CONTROL — NOT FROZEN

## 1. Purpose

Define minimum evidence expectations for distinct claim families.

The matrix prevents evidence suitable for one claim type from being silently
used to establish a stronger claim type.

## 2. CT-01 — REPORTING_OR_ATTENTION

May be supported by:

- admitted media publication;
- admitted public statement;
- permitted public signal;
- monitored-source publication counts.

May establish:

- that something was reported;
- detected attention within monitored sources;
- change in publication activity.

Does not by itself establish:

- event truth;
- prevalence;
- impact;
- severity;
- causality.

## 3. CT-02 — ATTRIBUTED_ASSERTION

May be supported by:

- original statement;
- official transcript;
- attributable publication;
- direct documentary record.

May establish:

- that the identified actor made the assertion.

Does not by itself establish:

- that the assertion is factually correct.

## 4. CT-03 — EVENT_OCCURRENCE

Potential support:

- direct observation;
- official incident record;
- independent reporting;
- documentary record;
- measurement consistent with event occurrence.

Repeated publication from one origin is insufficient independent
corroboration.

An allegation alone does not establish event occurrence.

## 5. CT-04 — EMPIRICAL_CONDITION

Potential support:

- measurement;
- administrative record;
- structured dataset;
- direct documented observation;
- qualified investigation.

Media volume alone is insufficient.

Source popularity alone is insufficient.

## 6. CT-05 — FREQUENCY_OR_PREVALENCE

Requires a defensible observation basis including, where applicable:

- denominator;
- sampling/coverage definition;
- observation method;
- comparability;
- appropriate time window.

Publication count is not a prevalence denominator.

Zero media mentions are not evidence of zero prevalence.

## 7. CT-06 — IMPACT_OR_SEVERITY

Potential support:

- documented affected population;
- measured damage;
- service impact;
- health/environmental/economic indicators;
- operational records;
- appropriately qualified investigation.

Sentiment volume and article volume are insufficient by themselves.

## 8. CT-07 — NARRATIVE_OR_PERCEPTION

May be supported by:

- attributable public statements;
- admitted public discourse;
- valid surveys;
- permitted social/public signals;
- qualitative research.

A convenience sample of public posts must not be presented as
population-wide opinion.

## 9. CT-08 — INSTITUTIONAL_AUTHORITY

Potential support:

- law;
- regulation;
- formal mandate;
- official delegation;
- authoritative institutional documentation.

Geographic proximity alone is insufficient.

Issue relevance alone is insufficient.

## 10. CT-09 — OPERATIONAL_CAPABILITY

Potential support:

- documented operational responsibility;
- organisational capacity;
- service mandate;
- resource or response evidence;
- verified operational arrangements.

Legal authority alone does not prove practical capability.

## 11. CT-10 — CAUSAL_OR_STRUCTURAL

Requires stronger methodological justification.

Potential evidence may include:

- defensible causal study;
- mechanism evidence;
- counterfactual/reference-class support;
- competing-hypothesis assessment;
- temporal ordering;
- confounder analysis;
- disconfirming evidence.

Co-occurrence and temporal correlation are insufficient by themselves.

## 12. CT-11 — FORESIGHT_HYPOTHESIS

A foresight hypothesis must preserve:

- driver;
- mechanism;
- horizon;
- supporting evidence;
- assumptions;
- alternative explanations;
- contradictory evidence;
- leading indicators;
- watch conditions;
- invalidation/falsification conditions;
- uncertainty;
- human review state;
- point-in-time version.

A foresight hypothesis is not an established fact.

A scenario is not automatically a forecast.

No fabricated probability is permitted.

## 13. Negative-Evidence Rule

A claim of absence requires more than non-detection.

QUALIFIED_NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE requires a defensible basis that the observation
process would reasonably have detected the condition if present.

Otherwise use:

NO_OBSERVED_SIGNAL

or:

INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE.

## 14. Compound Claims

A compound claim must be decomposed when different parts require different
evidence.

Example:

"Flooding occurred across the city because drainage maintenance failed."

contains at least:

- EVENT_OCCURRENCE;
- FREQUENCY_OR_PREVALENCE / geographic extent;
- CAUSAL_OR_STRUCTURAL.

Evidence supporting the occurrence must not automatically support citywide
prevalence or causality.

## 15. Production Evidence Rule

Only appropriately admitted evidence may support production claims.

Z1 signals may trigger verification.

They may not become customer-facing production findings without Z2 support
for the relevant claim.

## 16. Qualification Boundary

This matrix defines epistemic sufficiency expectations.

It does not define final Issue qualification authority.

Human authority is governed by S0.5D.
